## 3. Scope va tracks cua hoi nghi

Website cong bo 9 nhom track lon. Voi de tai hien tai, cac track lien quan nhat la:

### Track 1: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Business Analytics

Noi dung phu hop nhat voi bai cua de tai:

- Deep learning algorithms and architectures
- AI-driven business analytics and decision support
- Natural language processing, knowledge systems and text analysis
- Predictive and prescriptive analytics in business
- Data visualization
- AI, ethics, and responsible governance

Danh gia:
- Day la track hop nhat neu bai duoc dong khung theo huong `LLM-based natural language interface for data querying`.
- Cum `Natural Language Processing, Knowledge Systems & Text Analysis` va `AI-Driven Business Analytics and Decision Support` rat hop voi bai NL2SQL.

### Track 9: Digital Transformation, Innovation & Cross-Disciplinary Topics

Noi dung co the dung de reframe bai:

- Business analytics in digital transformation journeys
- Simulation, modeling, and decision-making in business
- Ethics, responsible AI & cybersecurity analytics
- Emerging trends in applied business & economic research

Danh gia:
- Day la track du phong tot neu muon nhan manh gia tri ung dung cua he thong trong truy cap du lieu doanh nghiep bang ngon ngu tu nhien.
- Track nay hop hon neu bai viet theo goc nhin `digital transformation` hon la `NLP core`.

### Muc do phu hop cua de tai

De tai cua ban ve ban chat nam trong vung giao nhau giua:

- NLP / Text-to-SQL
- LLM agents / multi-agent orchestration
- Data access / decision support

Vi vay, de tai khop nhat voi:

1. `Track 1`
2. `Track 9`

Khuyen nghi:

  - `Text-to-SQL`

## 4. De tai NL2SQL nen duoc dong khung the nao de hop venue

Vi ten hoi nghi co chu `Business and Technology`, bai khong nen duoc dat nhu mot paper chi thuan benchmark ky thuat. Cach framing tot hon:

### Huong framing de xuat

`A Multi-Agent LLM Framework for Natural Language Access to Relational Business Data`

hoac

`Improving Natural Language-to-SQL Generation for Decision Support Using a Multi-Agent LLM Architecture`

### Cach mo ta dong gop

Nen nhan manh 4 y sau:

1. De xuat mot kien truc multi-agent cho bai toan sinh SQL tu ngon ngu tu nhien.
2. Tang do tin cay cua SQL generation thong qua phan tach vai tro: schema selection, SQL generation, validation.
3. Danh gia bang execution-oriented metrics thay vi chi so exact match.
4. Chi ra y nghia ung dung cho truy cap du lieu va ho tro ra quyet dinh trong he thong doanh nghiep.

### Nen tranh

- Khong frame bai nhu mot demo framework CrewAI don thuan.
- Khong noi qua nhieu ve giao dien, demo app, hay deployment neu khong co ket qua thuc nghiem ro.
- Khong de bai nghieng hoan toan sang ly thuyet semantic parsing ma mat lien he voi ung dung business/data access.

## 5.4. Review process

Theo `Review Process`:

- It nhat `2 independent referees`
- `single-blind`
- acceptance rate: `29%`
- similarity rate: `< 20%`
- AI similarity rate: `< 20%`

Luu y:
- Trang `Publication Opportunity` lai ghi `double-blind peer-review`
- Tuy nhien ban to chuc da tra loi email va yeu cau xem trang `Review Process`
- Vi vay, tam thoi coi `single-blind` la thong tin van hanh chinh

2. Neu chua dang ky, co the chon `virtual registration option` ngay khi dang ky.
3. Bai trinh bay online van `duoc dua vao conference proceedings va indexed duoi cung dieu kien nhu onsite`.
4. Chi tiet `Springer book series` se duoc chia se trong `copyright form` sau hoi nghi.
5. Ban to chuc yeu cau su dung `Springer Word template` tren website.

## 9. Danh gia tong hop cho de tai cua ban

## 9.1. Diem manh cua venue nay

- Con han nop bai.
- Da co xac nhan email cho phep `online presentation`.
- Bai online van duoc `publish/index` nhu onsite.
- Scope rong, co track AI/NLP/business analytics phu hop.
- Co gioi han trang ro rang (`12 pages`), de lap ke hoach rut gon ban thao.
- Phi `virtual + publication` chap nhan duoc so voi mot venue quoc te.

## 9.2. Diem can than trong

- Chua ro `Springer series` cu the truoc khi nop.
- Chi co Word template cong khai.
- Co su khong dong nhat nho giua mot vai trang tren website.
- Venue nghieng ve `Business and Technology`, nen bai can duoc frame theo huong ung dung, khong nen qua "benchmark-only".

## 9.3. Ket luan

`ICBTIstanbul 2026` la mot venue `du kha thi` cho de tai hien tai, dac biet trong boi canh:

- Ban khong the tham du offline.
- Venue da xac nhan online presenter van duoc proceedings/indexing.
- Bai cua ban co the duoc dat vao Track 1 va frame theo huong `AI-enabled natural language access to business data`.

## 10.4. Cau truc 12 trang de xuat

Mot khung hop ly:

1. Introduction
2. Related Work
3. Problem Formulation
4. Proposed Multi-Agent Architecture
5. Experimental Setup
6. Results and Discussion
7. Limitations / Threats to Validity
8. Conclusion

Phan bo tham khao:

- Introduction: 1-1.5 trang
- Related Work: 1-1.5 trang
- Method: 2-2.5 trang
- Experimental Setup: 1.5 trang
- Results + Analysis: 2-3 trang
- Conclusion: 0.5 trang
- References: 1-1.5 trang

## 10.5. Nhung bang/figure nen co

- 1 figure kien truc multi-agent
- 1 bang so sanh single-agent vs multi-agent
- 1 bang so sanh metric theo benchmark
- 1 bang error analysis theo nhom loi

## 11. Ke hoach hanh dong de nop venue nay

### Buoc 1. Chot scope bai

Chi giu lai dong gop thuc su co ket qua:

- baseline single-agent
- de xuat multi-agent
- schema-aware / validation-aware pipeline
- execution-oriented evaluation

### Buoc 2. Rut gon bai ve khung 12 trang

Can uu tien:

- ket qua dinh luong
- related work sat chu de
- bang so sanh ro rang

Can cat bot neu can:

- mo ta implementation qua chi tiet
- noi dung demo/UI
- cac y tuong chua co thuc nghiem day du

### Buoc 3. Chuan bi dinh dang nop

- Viet nhap noi bo bang Markdown/LaTeX neu thuan tien
- Chuyen sang `Springer Word template` de nop
- Kiem tra similarity va AI similarity < 20%
- Chuan hoa references theo mot style thong nhat

### Buoc 4. Luu bang chung trao doi email

Luu email tra loi cua ban to chuc de dung khi can:

- xac nhan virtual participation
- xac nhan online paper van duoc proceedings/indexing
